A trading fee rebate (cashback) gives you back a share of the fees you pay. But a rebate is a percentage of the fees, so a bigger percentage on a pricier exchange can still cost you more. These tables rank exchanges by **net cost**: fees minus cashback, at the fee tier your monthly volume reaches. Every order is taken as taker.
